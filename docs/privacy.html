Privacy Policy

MS BEAU AVE Enterprises OPC · Last updated: August 2026

This policy covers the MS BEAU AVE website, the MS BEAU AVE customer app, and the MBA Back Office app (together, "the Apps").

Information we collect

  • Account & membership details you provide: name, mobile number, and loyalty membership information.
  • Order details: items purchased, delivery or pickup preference, payment method and reference number, and order status history.
  • Bookings: appointment details you submit for in-store services such as skin analysis.

We do not collect passwords, card numbers, e-wallet credentials, precise location, contacts, photos, or any data from your device beyond what you type into the Apps.

How we use it

  • To process and fulfil your orders, and to let you track deliveries.
  • To run the loyalty programme (earning and redeeming points).
  • To manage bookings and notify you about them.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties except the service providers that host our systems (website hosting and database services) — and they process it only on our behalf.

Payments

Payments via GCash, Maya, bank transfer, or other channels are completed inside those providers' own apps. We store only the reference number you give us to confirm the payment. We never see or store your e-wallet or bank credentials.

Data retention & your rights

We keep order and membership records for as long as needed to operate the loyalty programme and comply with Philippine bookkeeping requirements. Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173), you may request a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Contact us and we will act on your request.

Children

The Apps are general-audience shopping tools and are not directed at children under 13.
